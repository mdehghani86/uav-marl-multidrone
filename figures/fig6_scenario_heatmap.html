Figure 6. Which method wins each scenario
Each method's wins across the 36-scenario grid. Grey cells = method lost that scenario. Coloured cells = method won.
n = 50 evaluation episodes per scenario · 14,400 episodes total · matched layout seeds · 36 scenarios = 3 fleet sizes × 3 customer counts × 2 spatial × 2 urgency
Figure 6. H4 greedy EDF wins most cells (18/36, or 50%) but its wins concentrate at small customer counts where coordination barely matters. H3 iterative auction wins only 28% of cells (10/36) yet leads the mean return (Fig 1) because its wins land in the hardest cases — large \(N\), tight deadlines, clustered layouts — where the reward differential is largest. H1 and H2 occupy narrow niches.
