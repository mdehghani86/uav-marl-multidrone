Figure 5. Drones \(\{D_1, \ldots, D_M\}\) leave depot \(v_0\) to serve customers before their deadlines; a charger \(c_1\) can restore battery en route. At each decision step exactly one drone acts — the one with the smallest local clock — and chooses a next node from its feasibility-filtered action mask (battery- and time-reachable, unvisited). The 36-cell grid varies M, N, spatial distribution, and deadline urgency.
